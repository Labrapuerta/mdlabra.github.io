About me

I’m interested in the point where applied mathematics becomes useful, efficient software—and in understanding every layer in between.

Learning through building.

I work across data science, research software, and computational biology. My main project is biomedical GraphRAG, where I focus on implementation, optimization, evidence traceability, and evaluation over biological knowledge graphs.

I’m happiest when I can connect theory with implementation: opening the box, rebuilding an idea from first principles, and turning a difficult problem into a clear, testable system.

Based in Barcelona, I’m building a portfolio that will continue to grow with new experiments, research, and software.
